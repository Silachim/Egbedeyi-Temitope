# Phase 2 Research Page Batch

This is a targeted Research-page batch. It does not replace your completed homepage or About page.

## Add/replace these files

- `src/pages/Research.jsx`
- `src/data/researchContent.js`
- `src/styles/research-page.css`

`Research.jsx` imports `research-page.css` directly, so you do not need to replace `global.css`.

## What is included

- Research-program hero
- Four connected research strands
- Current and selected research projects
- Methods & approaches
- Externally funded research
- Research collaborators & scholarly network
- Research-informed applications
- Research insights
- Selected research outputs
- Closing pathways

## Notes

- The collaborators section uses the names and affiliations represented in the current site screenshots. Review them before production deployment and update any affiliation that has changed.
- NSF and Jaffe funding are explicitly presented as funded projects on which Temitope serves/served as a Research Assistant or research team member, not as personal grants.
- Multiply Master and Naija Trivia remain under research translation. Noted was intentionally left out because it has a weaker direct connection to the mathematics research program.
- Hash-route links are preserved to match the existing React routing architecture.
